Changelog (13/10/2026):

=== Device changes ===

Battery & charging
- Standby drain fixed: an unused Qualcomm video service kept the DSP awake. Screen-off drain is now ~0.6%/h with Wi-Fi and data on.
- Deeper sleep: fixed a CPU idle-state latency typo and switched off unused PCIe controllers and the NPU bandwidth vote while sleeping (from HeliumStudio-Dev).
- USB PD / PPS fast charging works on PD chargers (tested: Samsung 25W, Anker 140W). The charge pump now runs on the Qualcomm PSY driver, and the legacy charging attributes are back (from XiaoYeZi / HeliumStudio-Dev).
- Fast charging no longer drops and reconnects every ~35 seconds on high-wattage PPS chargers: requests are capped at the 9V the input can take.
- Charge-pump safety: the charge pump stops just below the 4.40V battery limit and the main charger finishes, so an aged battery can't trip the over-voltage cutoff.
- New: Bypass charging (Settings > Battery > Bypass charging, plus a Quick Settings tile). The charger powers the phone while the battery is left alone, for example while gaming on the charger. It is unavailable while Charging control is on.
- Charging control (charge limit and scheduled charging) is available in Settings > Battery.
- Battery percentage on worn batteries: the fuel gauge can now learn the battery's real capacity. After a full, uninterrupted charge that starts at 15% or lower, the percentage tracks the real charge instead of shutting down early at "30%".
- The fuel gauge no longer reports a bogus battery health at boot.

Performance
- App CPU grouping fixed: all apps were stuck in one CPU group. Top-app, foreground and background placement works again.
- Touch boost floors restored, and GPU limits now use the GPU driver's own power levels.
- The display wakes early before a frame and reports when it goes idle, so interaction boosts end on time.
- Fixed a kernel memory warning from ART's garbage collector.

Audio & Bluetooth
- Bluetooth headphones and earbuds reconnect on their own after being switched off and on.
- Wired headsets are kept off the raw audio output.

Device
- Pop-up camera LED lights up again when the selfie camera pops up.
- Power-off alarm works with any clock app.
- Fingerprint: the in-display sensor's pressed layer no longer forces GPU composition.

Logs
- Much less log spam (telephony satellite checks, Wi-Fi daemon, diag, charging drivers).

Kernel
- The ROM ships with the non-root kernel by default. If you need the KernelSU-Next kernel, flash it from: https://github.com/erg-raphael-5-4/android_kernel_xiaomi_sm8150/releases

=== Source changes (DerpFest / LineageOS) ===

- October 2026 security patch level.
- Chromium WebView 155.0.8059.39.
- Now Playing song recognition on non-Pixel phones, shown on the lockscreen.
- Lockscreen: reworked ambient card and chip placement, blur fixes, UDFPS icon fixes (rotation, illumination dot, leaks).
- Dynamic Island / DynamicBar: call timer starts on connect, alert card actions, message notification fixes. Dynamic Island is disabled with an explanation on phones without a display cutout.
- Volume panel: per-app volume sliders grouped, with percentage announcements.
- Lyrics: LRCLIB as the default source, character highlighting, colon-separated timestamps.
- Pulse: custom color and height.
- Obscura: hide and lock apps, with an app control page.
- App spoofing: more CPU and GPU models, keybox exclusions per app, Play Integrity toggle fixes.
- Biometric fallback with a scrambled PIN layout.
- Boot animation follows Monet colours and the light or dark theme.
- DerpLauncher: app drawer sorting fixes, custom drawer background, Task Lock in recents, recents blur, private space and work profile fixes.
- Settings: Simple View accessibility option, single-band hotspot choices, configurable dual shade gesture split, wallpaper behind the About phone logo.
- Canvas replaces Gallery2 as the photo editor; it gets filters and tooltips.
- Updater: stale updates are cleared before checking, and major upgrades are offered as manual installs.
- Setup wizard uses the new expressive theme. Accessibility gains Vision Settings.
- Face unlock: enrolment shows a progress ring and handles rotation better.
- Recovery: menu redrawn as a rounded group.
- Fixes for MMS PDU parsing, remote WAP PDU denial of service, and power HAL session crashes.
- APN updates and other LineageOS upstream fixes.
