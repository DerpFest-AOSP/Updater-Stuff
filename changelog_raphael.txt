Changelog (01/10/2026):

Camera
- MIUI Camera (Leica 5.0 port) is now the camera app: photo with 0.6x / 1x / 2x lens switching, portrait, panorama, night, pro, documents, video, and slow motion at 120 and 240 fps.
- Known issue: the 1080p slow motion preview can stutter.

Calls
- WhatsApp, Telegram and other VoIP call audio no longer drops out for 5-10 seconds when a notification sound plays.

Updates
- OTA updates from the built-in Updater install again.

Performance
- Snapdragon 855+ units are no longer capped at 585 MHz on the GPU.
- Updated task profiles and display idle settings.

Apps
- Maps, Photos and Gmail are no longer preinstalled; install them from the Play Store if you need them.
- Accord is removed.

SystemUI
- SystemUI's new scene container is turned off.

Kernel
- The ROM ships with the non-root kernel by default. If you need the KernelSU-Next kernel, flash it from: https://github.com/erg-raphael-5-4/android_kernel_xiaomi_sm8150/releases
